# Platform and safety rules

Read this before a series, a generated host, a film built on someone else's footage, or anything that flashes. Platforms change their rules, and everything here was read at the source on 2026-11-02, so check the page before relying on a detail. Nothing here is legal advice. The labels are defined in [story.md](../../altocue-direct/references/story.md).

## Flashes

- **[evidence] Three flashes or fewer in any one second.** WCAG 2.2, criterion 2.3.1, Level A. The working rule lives in `altocue-transitions`.

## YouTube's monetisation rules

**[evidence]** These come from [YouTube's channel monetisation policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392). They decide whether a channel can earn. They do not remove videos. Reviewers look at the channel as a whole: its main theme, most viewed videos and newest videos. So a film is only as safe as the channel around it, and no gate can clear one. Altocue can warn. Tell the user.

| Rule | What it names | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Inauthentic content. A notice of July 15, 2025 renamed the repetitious content policy. On the page itself, look for Generic or Repetitive Content | Channels whose videos feel interchangeable. Characters put in the same situation over and over with the same outcome. Image slideshows or scrolling text with minimal narrative or educational value. AI content from generic templates with no original insight | Keep a format, and give each film a distinct idea. A shared intro and outro are fine. A series with one cast is fine when each film has its own story |
| Reused content, even with permission | Show clips edited together with little narrative. Short videos compiled from other social sites | Own footage by default. Substantive editing with added commentary or story is allowed. A permission note does not clear this, and copyright applies separately |
| Off-putting content | Repeated disturbing themes without a real story. Unrelated AI clips stitched together to shock. Realistic visuals that trick viewers about a death or disaster | Do not make these |
| AI personas on sensitive topics | Channels using AI personas as human experts on health, legal issues, finances or politics, such as wellness remedies or financial guidance | A generated host never advises. See `altocue-ugc` |

**AI itself is not the problem.** The page allows AI for a unique character and story, for script editing and for original background visuals.

## Advice to refuse

These turn up in guides, and none of them is safe to repeat:

- **Claims that AI output cannot be struck for reuse.** YouTube's page says otherwise.
- **Publishing on a schedule with no person looking.** It runs straight at the mass-production language above, and Altocue does not publish.
- **Earnings, view counts and agency prices quoted in guides.** Treat them as marketing unless the user can show their own.
