# Story craft for short-form character reels

Research-backed (Aug 2026). The physics: the algorithm's first distribution
decision lands in ~1.5 seconds; ~71% of viewers decide in the opening
seconds; 80-85% of Reels play MUTED, so every reel must work as a silent
film; under 15s, a seamless loop multiplies completion, the strongest
distribution signal. The north star for brand-character work is Sylvanian
Drama (3.5M followers, 16M-view brand spots): "the less you mention the
brand, the better." The target comment is "wait, this is an ad?"

## The six story shapes

Pick ONE shape per reel. A reel that explains two things explains nothing at
silent-scroll speed.

1. **Overwhelm-to-Exhale (12-20s).** 0-1.5s chaos already in motion; 2-8s
   escalation in 2-3 cuts; 8-14s the turn (one small intervention, often a
   second character or the world itself); 14-18s the EXHALE, held 2 full
   seconds of stillness after motion. For mental-load validation.
2. **Two-Hands Handoff (8-15s).** One character mid-struggle with a
   two-character task; the second pair of hands arrives WITHOUT being asked;
   the task turns easy, almost musical. Close on the two characters, not the
   task. For both-parent accountability; alternate who carries and who
   arrives.
3. **Five Small Things (15-25s).** Countable ritual: five beats ~3s each,
   visibly counted (lanterns, pebbles, petals lighting one by one), then the
   wide showing five small things made one good day. Highly save-able.
4. **Hard Day That Counts (12-20s).** A plan visibly fails; the character
   sits in it (do NOT rescue too fast; let the sad beat breathe); then
   reframe, not fix: the day still gets the identical mark as a good day.
   Close on the mark, not a smile. Highest-share category for parents.
5. **Invisible List Revealed (10-18s).** Mundane action; pull back / overlay
   reveals everything silently tracked (threads, a hidden ledger); a second
   character SEES it for the first time; hold the reaction. The tag-your-
   partner shape.
6. **Seamless Loop (8-12s).** A cyclical ritual where the last frame IS the
   first. Direct the ending first so it match-cuts into the opening. For
   ambient world content and watch-count multiplication.

## Hook taxonomy (no talking heads)

The top-performing hook class is the visual outcome drop-in: zero setup, the
payoff already in frame one.

- **Mid-action drop-in**: open inside the moment; never establish first.
- **Wrong-scale object**: tiny character, absurdly big list/pile. Instantly
  legible on mute; says "mental load" without a word.
- **POV text overlay**: "POV: you're the parent who remembered the water
  bottle." Casts the viewer as the character.
- **Confession card**: one specific, slightly raw truth as text ("nobody
  claps when you remember the snack"); the scene answers it. Specific beats
  clever.
- **Pattern break**: one discordant element in the soft world (rain indoors,
  one item out of place); resolve it at the end (open loop).
- **Count promise**: "5 things" / "day 12" on frame one. Serialized formats
  earn ~2.3x engagement vs standalone.

Never open with: a logo, an establishing wide, a character waving hello, or
any "let me tell you about" equivalent.

## Concept-without-product rules

1. Dramatize the RELIEF, not the mechanism (sell the exhale, not the
   checklist).
2. The concept appears as PHYSICS of the world: magic that behaves the way
   the product behaves.
3. One concept per reel.
4. Fixed character registers: dynamics stay constant across every reel so
   accountability stories write themselves as relationship beats (the
   Duolingo lesson: bend the trend to the character, never the character to
   the trend).

## Never do

- Guilt framing (91% of mothers already report guilt; backlash is real).
  Failure is weather, never character flaw.
- "Enjoy it while it lasts" sentimentality (reads as a threat).
- Perfect-parent contrast (no honor roll in the world).
- App UI, phones, notification sounds inside the story world.
- Mom-only framing of care work.
- Engagement-bait CTAs ("comment YES", "tag a mom who...") - platform-
  suppressed.
- Breaking the character's emotional register for a trend.

## Loop, caption, CTA mechanics

- **Text overlays**: max one line at a time; first line readable within
  1.5s; ~3s per card; high contrast; safe zones; the reel works with zero
  audio.
- **Caption is the second act**: the video validates, the caption converts
  quietly. Formula: one resonant line extending the story + one soft
  identity line + searchable keywords ("mental load", "default parent",
  "parenting as a team"). Short-video views correlate with branded search on
  a ~7-day lag.
- **CTA norms**: open-ended questions are safe ("what counted today?");
  route traffic via profile and a keyword-rich pinned comment; keep 80-90%
  of reels CTA-free. Being sought out beats asking.
- **Seed the search**: end some reels on an unresolved detail so comments
  ask "what is this from?"; answer in character. The audience asking is the
  highest-trust brand mention there is.
